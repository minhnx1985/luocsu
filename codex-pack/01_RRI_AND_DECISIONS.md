# 01 — RRI & DECISIONS LOG

## D01 Scope
Published: 12 Vietnamese titles.
Coming soon: **A Little History of Art — Charlotte Mullins**.
Exclude Poetry and Earth.

## D02 Commerce
No cart. No combo.
Published books may link to Nhã Nam and/or Shopee.
Never invent a Shopee URL.
Never hard-code stock state.

## D03 Audience
Primary:
- age 16–35;
- curious general reader;
- wants a reliable entry point to a broad field;
- does not need specialist background.

Secondary:
- parents;
- teachers;
- general nonfiction readers.

Do not position the series primarily as children’s books.

## D04 Book card/detail
Card:
- cover
- title
- author
- one-line hook
- forthcoming state if relevant

Detail:
- cover
- Vietnamese title
- English title
- author
- translator if verified
- 2–3 sentence pitch
- 3–5 “Bạn sẽ gặp” cues
- sample
- Nhã Nam CTA
- Shopee CTA only when verified

Do not surface page count, trim size, publisher, publication year unless scope changes.

## D05 Assets
Local Vietnamese covers are the primary visual DNA.
Local PDFs are production samples.
Remote URLs may be recorded as provenance only.

## D06 Visual
Use:
- warm ivory/paper
- black editorial typography
- line-art / woodcut / linocut-like illustration
- one accent per volume
- restrained literary/reference-book feel

Do not clone Yale 2025 redesign.

## D07 Samples
Load PDF samples on demand, not all at initial render.

## D08 Technical
Near-static.
Data-driven.
No backend/CMS required unless existing workspace unexpectedly provides one.

## D09 Language
Vietnamese UI/copy.
English title appears only in supporting/detail metadata.

## D10 Future additions
Adding a title should mainly require adding/updating one data object and assets.

## Project
Build a near-static Vietnamese minisite for Nhã Nam's editions of Yale University Press's **Little Histories** series.

## Strategic proposition
# **Một bộ sách nhỏ cho những câu hỏi lớn.**

The minisite is a **knowledge map for curious readers**, not merely a product catalogue.

Core user jobs:
1. Understand what the series is.
2. Answer: “Tôi nên bắt đầu từ cuốn nào?”
3. Browse the full series.
4. Open details for one book.
5. Read a sample PDF.
6. Continue to Nhã Nam/Shopee to purchase.

## Execution mode
**AUTONOMOUS ONE-SHOT**

The Builder must proceed:
SCAN → validate Vision → normalize assets/data → BUILD → VERIFY → REFINE → DONE.

No intermediate Human checkpoint unless a Level-3 blocker occurs.

## Human-approved scope
- 12 titles already published in Vietnamese.
- 1 forthcoming title: `A Little History of Art — Charlotte Mullins`.
- Poetry and Earth excluded.
- No combo/cart.
- Vietnamese UI.
- Audience primarily 16–35 curious non-specialists.
- High-resolution Vietnamese covers already exist in the workspace.
- Sample PDFs already exist in the workspace.
- Visual direction comes from the Vietnamese Nhã Nam covers.
- Site is nearly static and data-driven.
